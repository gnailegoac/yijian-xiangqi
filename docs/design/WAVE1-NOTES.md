### G2

- **6.1 (at most one live task per channel) with 6.3 step 3 (abort listeners run synchronously)** — Not stated what happens when an abort listener of the task being superseded itself starts a run task on the same channel, or cancels everything, while the superseding run() is still in progress. **Reading:** The call made inside the listener is the later one and wins. Every undelivered job message on the live worker counts, the expected `cancelled` acknowledgement included; every message of a terminated worker counts; a repeated `ready` on the live worker does not (now asserted). So lateDrops is above 0 after any ordinary cancel.
- **6.5 (spawn failure)** — "Retry after 0 ms, 250 ms, 1000 ms; the third consecutive failure marks the engine dead": with death at the third failure only two retries happen, so 1000 ms is never used. **Reading:** The third consecutive failure is final, matching the 14.4 title 'three failed spawns mark the engine dead' and the design prototype (same table, dead at three). - **6.3, 6.5, 4.8.1 (job timeoutMs)** — Not stated whether a job's timeoutMs runs while the job is held (before `ready`, or during a respawn). **Reading:** The timeout runs only while the worker has the job: it starts at the post and starts again in full when the job is re-posted after a respawn (the prototype did the same). A held job ends through the spawn-failure path, at worst after about 15 s (three 5000 ms limits plus 0 and 250 ms).
- **3.8 RunContext, 6.7, 5.8 (tasks.run)** — Not stated when `fn` is invoked, or whether the signal aborts when a run task ends by itself. **Reading:** `fn` is called synchronously inside run(), so the request leaves at once and the director can capture the reply object. - **4.19 onWorker 'respawn'** — The event name is in the signature but section 6 never says when it fires. **Reading:** Reported once each time a greeted worker is terminated and replaced (150 ms backstop, job timeout, onerror / onmessageerror), after the affected job is settled and before the replacement is created. - **6.5 (onWorker 'ready' and held jobs; onWorker 'dead' and live jobs)** — The order of the callback relative to the job effects is not stated. **Reading:** On `ready` the held job is posted first, then onWorker('ready', info) is called (not pinned by a test). - **4.19 dispose(), restart()** — Behaviour after dispose() and of restart() on a live engine is not described. **Reading:** dispose() cancels every live task ('cancelled'), terminates the worker and clears all timers. Afterwards job() and run() return handles already resolved with 'cancelled' and `fn` is not called. restart() does nothing unless the engine is dead.
